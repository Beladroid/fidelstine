The safety and dignity of the children and vulnerable adults we serve comes before everything else, including fundraising.

## Photos, videos and stories

- We only publish photos and videos of a child with written consent from a parent, guardian or the home's director.
- We never publish a child's full name, school name, or the exact location of the home alongside their picture.
- Where consent is missing, we photograph hands, activities or backs, or we blur faces.
- We remove location data from every photo before it goes online.
- Stories are told with dignity. We do not use images designed to shame or pity.
- If a child or guardian changes their mind, we remove the picture.

## Staff and volunteers

- Everyone who works with children is checked before they start and is trained in our safeguarding procedures.
- No volunteer is left alone with a child without proper checks and supervision.
- Visitors to the home must arrange their visit in advance and are accompanied at all times.

## Raising a concern

If you are worried about the safety or welfare of a child connected to Fidelstine, please tell us straight away using the [contact form](/contact/) or by phone. Every concern is taken seriously, handled confidentially, and reported to the proper authorities where required.

If a child is in immediate danger, contact the police or local emergency services first.

## Removal requests

If you see an image on this site that you believe should not be here, please [contact us](/contact/) and we will review it within 48 hours.
