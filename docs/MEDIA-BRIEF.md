# Filming and photo brief

This brief is for anyone at Fidelstine who takes photos or videos for the website. Following it means new footage can go straight onto the site without editing.

## Before you film anyone

- Get permission from every adult you film, and from a guardian or the home's director for every child.
- Do not show a child's full name, school name, or the home's exact address in the shot or in the caption.
- If you do not have consent for a child, film from behind, film hands and activities, or tell us so we can blur faces.
- Keep a simple consent record: date, event, who agreed.

## What we need, and where it goes on the site

| Type | Shape | Length | Sound | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Background loop | Landscape 16:9 | 10-20 seconds | Not needed | Top of pages, full-width bands behind text |
| Vertical clip | Portrait 9:16, phone held upright | 15-60 seconds | Optional | "Stories" reel that visitors tap through, phone hero |
| Story film | Landscape 16:9 | 1-3 minutes | Yes, clear speech | Video library |
| Testimonial | Landscape or portrait | About 1 minute | Yes, quiet place | Testimonials |
| Photos | Both landscape and portrait | | | Gallery, slideshows, programme pages |
| Before and after | Two photos, same place, same angle | | | Before/after slider |

## Tips for good footage

- **Hold still.** Rest the phone on something or hold it with both hands. Background loops should have calm, slow movement.
- **Light behind you.** Face people toward the light, not away from it.
- **No text on screen.** The website adds its own headings on top of video. Flyers with text burned in do not resize well.
- **Highest quality setting.** 1080p or 4K is fine. We compress it for the site.
- **Leave space.** For background loops, keep the main subject off to one side so headings have room.
- **Quiet for speech.** For testimonials, film indoors or away from generators and traffic.

## Useful subjects

- Daily life at the home: meals, homework, play, prayer, chores
- Lessons and vocational training
- Relief distributions: food, clothing, medical care
- Christmas Scheme preparation and delivery
- Staff and volunteers at work
- The buildings and grounds, without identifying signs if the address should stay private

## How to send files

Send the original files, not WhatsApp-forwarded copies, because WhatsApp shrinks them. Google Drive, WeTransfer or a USB drive all work. For each file, add one line saying what it shows, where, when, and whether consent was given.
