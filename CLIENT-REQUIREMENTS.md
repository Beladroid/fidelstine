## 2. Mission Statement

> At Fidelstine Charity Concerns and Orphanage, we exist to restore dignity to the abandoned and hope to the forgotten. We provide safe shelter, nourishing care, quality education, and unwavering emotional support to orphaned and vulnerable children and adults, not as charity alone, but as a sacred responsibility. Every child who walks through our doors is met with love, protection, and the tools to build a future of their own choosing. Through compassionate caregiving, family reunification, vocational training, empowerment, and crisis response, we stand in the gap for those society has overlooked, walking with them from vulnerability to self-sufficiency, one life at a time.

## 3. Vision Statement

> We envision a world where no child grows up unseen, unheard, or unloved, where every abandoned life is given a second chance at wholeness. Fidelstine Charity Concerns and Orphanage aspires to be a beacon of hope across Nigeria and beyond: a home where broken stories are rewritten, where trauma gives way to resilience, and where vulnerable children and adults are equipped not just to survive, but to thrive, lead, and give back to the communities that once overlooked them. We dream of a future built on dignity, justice, and belonging for every human life we touch.

## 4. Core Values

- **Compassion** — We treat every child, the less privileged, the abandoned and vulnerable adult with the tenderness, patience, and empathy they deserve, seeing beyond circumstance to the person within.
- **Dignity** — We uphold the worth of every individual in our care, ensuring they are never defined by their past, but empowered by their potential.
- **Integrity** — We operate with transparency and accountability in all we do, honoring the trust placed in us by donors, partners, and the communities we serve.
- **Resilience** — We equip those we serve with the emotional strength, education, and life skills to rise above trauma and build independent, fulfilling lives.
- **Community** — We believe healing happens in relationship, fostering family reunification, local partnerships, and a sense of belonging for every child.
- **Advocacy** — We speak up for the rights of the vulnerable, working to influence policy and practice that protects those who cannot protect themselves.
